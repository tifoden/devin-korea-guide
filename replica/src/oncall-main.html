I traced the queue buildup to the checkout consumers. Logs show 240 semaphore acquire timed out errors in the last 5 minutes. The deploy at 14:31 UTC raised the consumer batch size from 50 to 500, while the semaphore stayed at 50 slots. Batches are blocking on that limit and backing up the queue.~3% of checkout confirmations are delayed, none lost. Payments aren't failing. Marking this Sev-2 and paging @checkout-oncall.
